Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to trillypops.app@gmail.com.

It helps to add your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Sudrop version, the mode (Calm, Rush or Classic) and the board size. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

Digits fall one at a time, and you pick the column. A row, a column or a 3x3 box clears when it holds every digit exactly once (1 to 6 on the 6x6 board). Hold a finger on a column to see where the digit will land, then let go to drop it. A short tutorial shows the basics on first launch; you can replay it any time from Settings.

Questions

What is a cracked tile?

If you drop a digit where it would repeat a number in its row, column or box, it cracks and blocks its cell. Clear a line next to it to shatter it. Before you let go, the preview shows a crack and the number it clashes with glows red, so you can slide to another column.

Why do stone rows appear?

Every few drops a row of stone pushes up from the bottom (a counter under the board shows when). Clears next to stones break them. The run ends when a rising row pushes a tile off the top.

What is the difference between Calm and Rush?

Calm has no timer: take as long as you like over every drop, while the digits get harder to place level by level. In Rush, digits fall on their own. Drop early to bank time for the next one; the speed climbs every 10 drops.

How do hints work?

In Calm and Rush, the Hint button points to a good column. You get a few hints per run. In Classic, a hint first explains the next logical step, for example "Only 7 fits in this cell", and then offers to place the number for you.

How do I switch between 9x9 and 6x6?

Settings > Board size. The new size applies to your next Calm or Rush run, so your current run is not lost. Classic is always 9x9.

What is Classic?

A third mode for quieter moments, with nothing falling: a plain 9x9 number grid. Tap a cell, then a number. There are four levels (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert), notes, undo, erase and hints, and no mistake limit. Every puzzle has one solution and can be solved by logic alone. In Classic options you can hide the timer, turn off the red mistake check, and choose whether notes are tidied up for you.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. Sudrop has no account and does not sync between devices. Your run is saved after every drop, and each Classic level keeps one saved puzzle, so you can stop and pick it up later. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too (an iPhone backup may bring it back when you restore that backup).

Can I turn off sound, vibration or motion?

Yes. Settings has Sound and Haptics switches, and Appearance (System, Light or Dark). Sudrop follows your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting (Settings > Accessibility > Motion).

Does Sudrop collect data or show ads?

No. Sudrop has no ads, no tracking and no account, and it works offline. See the privacy policy.
