Sudrop

Digits fall. You choose where they land.

Each new digit drops onto the board and you pick its column. A row, a column or a box clears when it holds every digit exactly once.

123456789
  • Repeat a digit and it cracks. Stone rows push up from below, and clears can chain.
  • Hold a finger on a column to see exactly where the digit will land, and what it would clash with.
  • Calm, with no timer, or Rush, where dropping early banks time.
  • A 9x9 board with 3x3 boxes, or a quicker 6x6 board.
  • Also inside: Classic, a quiet 9x9 number grid with four levels, notes and hints that explain.
  • No ads, no account, works offline, and your progress stays on your iPhone.
