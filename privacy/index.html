The short version

Sudrop does not collect any data about you. There is no account and no sign-in, and the game does not connect to the internet. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game saves on your iPhone: your current run in Calm and in Rush, your saved Classic puzzle for each level, your best scores, your Classic best and average times, your settings (appearance, board size, sound, haptics, Classic options, whether you have seen the tutorial), and a short history of your finished runs (for example score, number of drops and how long the run took), which only the game itself uses. This is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data with it.

Apple's own device backups (iCloud or computer backups) may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. That is between you and Apple; we do not receive it.

What Sudrop does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No network requests from the game. It works fully offline.

Third-party components

Sudrop is built with the Unity game engine. In this version, Unity's online services (analytics, crash reporting, hardware statistics and ads) are switched off, and the app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or payment software.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit it, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on this site. The game itself has no links to it.

Information from Apple

Apple may collect information about downloads, crashes and usage under your own Apple settings (for example "Share with App Developers" under Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements); Apple's privacy policy covers that. From Apple we only receive crash reports and summary statistics that do not identify you.

Children

Sudrop is a puzzle game for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under laws such as the EU and UK GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because Sudrop does not collect personal data, we hold none about you. You can remove everything the game saved by deleting the app. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it when the conversation is done. For any privacy question or request, contact Hasan Bayhan at trillypops.app@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

A future version may add features such as an advertising banner. Some of these may involve processing data (for example by an advertising partner). Before any such version is released, we will update this policy to say exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and update the App Store privacy information. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version of this policy.
